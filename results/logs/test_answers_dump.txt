===== t03 How do courts decide how long an injunction should last?
  EXP: Based on the time it would take a competitor to reveal the trade secret by proper means; it can end when the secret ceases to exist.
  [baseline_dense] answered ctx=['p185-c01', 'p185-c02', 'p184-c02'] cites=['p185-c01', 'p185-c02', 'p184-c02'] :: Courts decide the duration of an injunction based on the time required for independent development of the misappropriated information or knowledge, often relying on expert opinions and competitor experiences [C-openstax-intro-ip-2021-p185-c01]. Furthermore, under the UTSA, an injunction may continue for a reasonable period to eliminate any commercial advantage from the misappropriation, even if the information is no longer a trade secret [C-openstax-intro-ip-2021-p184-c02].
